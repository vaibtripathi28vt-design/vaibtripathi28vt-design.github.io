02 / CATCHMENT About

VT
Currently

Doctoral Researcher
Dept. of Water Resources Development & Management
Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee, India

I am a PhD student working at the intersection of hydrology, hydroclimatology, and artificial intelligence. My research explores how climate, catchment characteristics, and human activities shape hydrological extremes and streamflow dynamics across river basins.

I'm particularly interested in developing hydrological forecasting systems that combine physical understanding with advanced machine-learning and deep-learning techniques — mapping floodplains over data-scarce regions, characterising flood processes, and building short- to sub-seasonal streamflow forecasts with rigorous uncertainty quantification, all in service of better water-resource management under a changing climate.

I'm always open to discussing research ideas, collaborations, and opportunities in hydrology, climate science, and data-driven environmental modelling.

05 / BASIN Research Domain

What the water is asked to reveal

Eight threads, one basin — physical hydrology braided with data-driven modelling

Hydroclimatic Extremes

Characterising how compounding climate and catchment drivers push rivers into extreme flood and low-flow states.

Flood Inundation Modelling

Simulating and mapping flood extent and depth across large, often data-scarce, river basins.

Streamflow Forecasting

Short- to sub-seasonal discharge forecasting with deep sequence models and formal uncertainty quantification.

Remote Sensing & Geospatial Hydrology

Fusing satellite observation — historical and contemporary — with GIS to track basin change over decades.

Ungauged Basin Hydrology

Reconstructing discharge and catchment behaviour where no gauge record exists, at national scale.

Explainable AI for Hydrology

Opening the black box — attributing model predictions back to physically meaningful catchment drivers.

Monsoon-Driven Catchment Hydrology

Studying how the Indian monsoon's spatial and temporal variability propagates into catchment response.

Climate Change & Water Resources

Translating climate projections into actionable signals for long-term water-resource planning.
